# 🚲 Bike Sharing Data Analysis & Executive Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes a bike-sharing rental dataset using **Microsoft Excel** to uncover customer behavior, environmental influences, and operational demand patterns. The project follows a complete analytics workflow—from data cleaning and exploratory analysis to interactive dashboards and business recommendations.

The analysis is divided into three analytical datasets, followed by two business dashboards that present insights for different audiences.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze bike rental demand patterns.
- Understand customer behavior (Casual vs Registered users).
- Study the impact of weather and environmental conditions on rentals.
- Identify peak operational hours and weekday trends.
- Design interactive dashboards for business decision-making.
- Generate actionable business recommendations.

# 📈 Dataset Analyses

## 📌 Dataset 1 – Environmental Analysis

This analysis focuses on how environmental conditions affect bike rental demand.

### Visualizations

- Rental Demand by Temperature
- Rental Demand by Humidity
- Rental Demand by Wind Speed
- Rental Demand by Weather Conditions
- Seasonal Rental Distribution

### Key Insights

- Moderate temperatures resulted in the highest rental demand.
- Rental activity declined as humidity increased.
- Clear weather encouraged more rentals than rainy conditions.
- Wind speed had a relatively smaller influence compared to temperature and humidity.

---

## 📌 Dataset 2 – Customer Behavior Analysis

This section analyzes customer types and rental patterns.

### Visualizations

- Customer Composition
- Casual vs Registered Rentals
- Rental Distribution by Customer Type
- Customer Usage Trends
- Customer Comparison Charts

### Key Insights

- Registered users contributed the majority of rentals.
- Casual users showed more variation based on weather.
- Customer behavior differed significantly across environmental conditions.

---

## 📌 Dataset 3 – Operational Demand Analysis

This section focuses on daily operational trends.

### Visualizations

- Hourly Rental Trend
- Hourly Customer Usage Pattern
- Weekday Performance
- Weather Impact on Customer Types
- Rental Demand Heat Map

### Key Insights

- Peak demand occurred during morning and evening commuting hours.
- Friday recorded the highest average rentals.
- Demand was lowest during early morning hours.
- Weekday rental behavior followed clear commuting patterns.

# 📌 Key Business Insights

- Registered users accounted for over **91%** of total rentals.
- Peak rental demand occurred around **5:00 PM**, indicating strong evening commuting activity.
- Friday recorded the highest average rental demand.
- Clear weather generated the highest rental activity.
- Moderate temperatures encouraged greater bike usage.
- Higher humidity generally reduced rental demand.
- Rental activity followed predictable weekday commuting patterns.

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

- Increase bike availability during morning and evening peak hours.
- Improve bike redistribution before high-demand periods.
- Use weather forecasts for proactive fleet planning.
- Expand membership campaigns to convert casual riders into registered users.
- Schedule maintenance during low-demand hours.
- Monitor weekday demand to optimize operational efficiency.
